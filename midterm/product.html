Waterproof Hoop Earrings

Rs. 2,200

Description

Lightweight hoops made for 24/7 wear. These earrings are waterproof and tarnish-resistant, so you can swim, shower, work out and sleep in them without taking them off.

Each pair is made from a stainless steel core with a thick gold-tone PVD coating. This coating is much harder than regular plating, which is why the color does not fade or turn your skin green, even with daily wear.

The hoops are light enough to forget you are wearing them. The hinged closure clicks shut securely, and the smooth inner edge will not catch on your hair or clothes.

Care

Rinse with clean water after the beach or pool and pat dry. Avoid harsh chemicals such as bleach. Store in the pouch provided when you are not wearing them.
